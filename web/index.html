      About the project
      
      
      An Arduino watches a running motor and stops it before anyone gets hurt or the machine is damaged: a person too close, overheating, abnormal vibration, a jam or a tilt. This page shows every reading live and controls the machine from the lab PC.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Hardware
      
      
        
        
      • Arduino Uno with a hardware-interrupt emergency stop and watchdog
        • 
        
      • 12 V geared DC motor, L298N driver (PWM speed) and a power relay
        • 
        
      • MPU6050 for vibration and tilt, DHT22 for temperature and humidity
        • 
        
      • HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor for people near the machine
        • 
        
      • 16×2 I2C LCD, status lights, buzzer, 12 V / 5 V SMPS
        •
